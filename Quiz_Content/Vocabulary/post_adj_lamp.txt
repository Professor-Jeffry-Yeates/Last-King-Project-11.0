casting flickering shadows across the stone walls
glowing softly with enchanted fire
adorned with intricate brass filigree
filled with scented oil that perfumes the air
hanging from a rusted iron chain
emitting a steady but very dim, golden light
covered in soot from years of use
shaped like a dragon’s claw clutching the base
set with colored glass that could paint the room in hues
humming faintly with magical energy
swinging gently in the drafty corridor
burning with a smokeless, plae-blue flame
decorated with runes that pulse with light
cracked and patched with bits of wire
reflecting torchlight off polished silver panels
with dripped wax spilled onto the ancient floor
surrounded by a halo of dancing sparks
illuminating hidden corners with a pale warm glow
flickering dimly as if alive with its own spirit
with dim light casting eerie patterns through a latticework shade
