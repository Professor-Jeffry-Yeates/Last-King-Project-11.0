woven with intricate geometric patterns
frayed at the edges from centuries of use
embroidered with golden thread and wizard symbols
stained with ancient, unidentifiable marks
covered in a fine layer of enchanted dust
adorned with the crest of a forgotten kingdom
shimmering faintly with magical energy
faded by sunlight and darkend by water
bordered with runes that glow in the torchlight
thick and plush, muffling the sound of footsteps
decorated with scenes of legendary battles
smelling faintly of incense and old parchment
patched in places with mismatched fabrics
curling up at the corners, as if alive
humming softly when stepped upon
marked with the sigil of a powerful wizard
bearing scorch marks from a dragon’s breath
damp and moldy from years underground
sparkling with tiny, embedded gemstones
rippling as if moved by an unseen breeze
