covered in spidery handwriting that fades at the edges
stained with ancient ink and splashes of wax
curling at the corners from age and damp
sealed with a crimson wax sigil
torn and patched with scraps of other scrolls
inscribed with cryptic runes in shimmering gold
brittle and yellowed, threatening to crumble at a touch
marked with the crest of a forgotten kingdom
bearing a faded map of hidden treasure
singed along the edges by careless flames
wrapped with a silken ribbon, tied in a careful knot
splattered with dried blood and mysterious stains
written in invisible ink, revealed only by torchlight
adorned with tiny sketches of mythical beasts
folded many times, the creases nearly splitting
humming faintly with magical energy
accompanied by the faint scent of incense
covered in hurried notes and frantic scribbles
seemingly protected by a faint, shimmering magical barrier
revealing a secret message when held up to the light and burned
