carved from gleaming white marble, polished smooth by time
draped in moss and lichen from centuries in a damp hall
cracked and weathered, missing a hand and foot
adorned with a crown of tarnished gold and precious gems
covered in ancient runes that spiral around its base
looming over the room with a stern, watchful gaze
chipped and scarred from forgotten battles
holding a sword aloft, forever poised for battle
veiled in cobwebs and dust, barely recognizable
surrounded by offerings of cobwebs and old bones
with eyes set with glittering sapphires
bearing scorch marks from dragonfire
wrapped in iron chains, as if to restrain its power
standing atop a pedestal engraved with heroic deeds
shrouded in a faint, magical mist
depicting a legendary hero in mid-stride
partially crushed, its head lying at its feet
radiating a subtle, otherworldly glow
embraced by clinging webs
with an outstretched hand, beckoning to passersby
