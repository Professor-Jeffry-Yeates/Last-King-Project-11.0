marked with winding trails and hidden paths
faded and torn at the edges from years of use
annotated with cryptic symbols and ancient runes
stained with watermarks and old ink blots
bordered by intricate designs of mythical beasts
revealing secret locations when held to the light
covered in a web of intersecting roads and rivers
adorned with a compass rose in shimmering gold
showing lands long vanished beneath the sea
curled at the corners from being rolled and unrolled
dotted with illustrations of monsters and treasures
inscribed with the names of forgotten kingdoms
humming faintly with magical energy
protected by a thin sheet of enchanted glass
bearing scorch marks from a dragon’s breath
accompanied by a legend written in an unknown tongue
decorated with faded banners and heraldic crests
revealing a hidden route when burned by torchlight
covered in dust and cobwebs from years underground
depicting a labyrinthine dungeon beneath a ruined castle
