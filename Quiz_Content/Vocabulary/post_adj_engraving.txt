etched with ancient runes that glow faintly in the dark
bordered by intricate patterns of intertwining vines
worn smooth by centuries of passing hands
filled with gold leaf that catches the torchlight
depicting a legendary battle between dragons and knights
inscribed with a cryptic letters in an unknown tongue
marred by deep scratches and scorch marks
surrounded by symbols of forgotten kingdoms
shimmering with a subtle, magical aura
chipped and weathered, barely legible
adorned with tiny gemstones set into the stone
covered in moss and lichen from years underground
carved so finely it seems almost lifelike
hidden behind a layer of dust and cobwebs
sealed with a wax sigil of a lost kingdom
spiraling outward in a mesmerizing pattern
accompanied by the scent of old incense
depicting a map of lands long vanished
seemingly protected by a faint, shimmering barrier
revealing a secret message when touched by torchlight
