inscribed with ancient runes that shimmer in the torchlight
cracked and weathered by centuries of neglect
bordered by intricate carvings of mythical beasts
covered in moss and lichen from years underground
etched with a map of forgotten realms
glowing faintly with magical energy
chipped at the corners, pieces long lost
sealed with a wax sigil of a vanished order
adorned with gold leaf that catches the light
marred by deep scratches and scorch marks
hidden beneath a layer of dust and cobwebs
engraved with a cryptic prophecy
bearing the crest of an ancient king
humming softly when touched
set with tiny gemstones along its edge
partially buried in the roots of a great tree root
seemingly protected by a faint, shimmering barrier
depicting scenes of legendary battles
covered in strange symbols no scholar can decipher
revealing a secret message when bathed in torchlight
