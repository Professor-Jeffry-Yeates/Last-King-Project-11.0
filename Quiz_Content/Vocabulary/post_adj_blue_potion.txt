covered in dust
chipped at the rim
clouded with age
sealed with crumbling wax
stained by ancient liquids
wrapped in faded labels
corked and brittle
etched with worn markings
crusted with old residue
heavy and thick-walled
green glass, dulled by time
leaning slightly to one side
mottled with age spots
bearing a faded emblem
filled with mysterious sediment
cracked but intact
wrapped in cobwebs
marked by long-lost hands
frosted by years in darkness
resting in a pool of dust
