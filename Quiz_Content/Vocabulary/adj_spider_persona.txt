cheerful and full of cackling laughter
kind-hearted and gentle (most of the time)
always ready with a warm and fangy smile
playful and curious, especially near dinner
helpful to everyone she meets and eats with
brimming with innocent joy and joy of the innocent
thoughtful and caring, serving human kind
quick to share and comfort with peacful sleep
soft-spoken and polite and never rushed
radiating pure happiness and sweet perfume
friendly and eager to help, especially to the lost
bubbling with excitement and wonder and venom
always offering a kind word and helpful thought
gentle-hearted and understanding of people
spreading joy and sticky webbing wherever she goes
full of bright, hopeful energy, never angry
delighting in making others smile just like her
patient and always willing to listen to pleas of mercy
overflowing with compassion, understanding, and love
bringing warmth and happiness to every room in the dungeon
